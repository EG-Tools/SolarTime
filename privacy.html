Privacy Policy

Solar Time is designed to work without creating an account or asking for your name, address, or payment details.

Solar Time은 회원가입 없이 사용할 수 있으며 이름, 주소, 결제정보를 직접 수집하지 않습니다. 아래 내용은 서비스 운영, 환경설정 저장, 방문 통계 및 향후 광고 제공 과정에서 처리될 수 있는 정보를 설명합니다.

1. Information handled by the site

Your browser may store local preferences such as language, country and time-zone choice, camera presets, visual options, and music state in local storage. These settings remain on your device and are used only to restore your experience. Solar Time does not receive them as a user profile.

Standard server and security logs may contain an IP address, request time, requested URL, user agent, response status, and diagnostic information. They are used to deliver the site, prevent abuse, investigate failures, and protect the service.

2. Analytics

Solar Time uses Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics to understand aggregate visits, device performance, and feature usage. Google Analytics remains in denied-consent mode until the applicable consent choice permits storage. Cloudflare may process limited technical data needed to deliver pages, measure aggregate performance, and protect the site. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising and cookies

Solar Time is connected to Google AdSense. After the site is approved and advertising is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses, and browsing information to select, limit, measure, and report ads. Depending on your region and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Learn how Google uses data on partner sites in Google's partner-sites notice.

Solar Time requests a cookie choice before consent-based analytics or advertising storage is enabled. Where additionally required for Google advertising in the EEA, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, a Google-certified consent platform may also be used. Browser settings may be used to block or remove cookies, although parts of the service or advertising may then work differently.

4. External services and links

Media files are delivered through Cloudflare. Optional music, source-credit, social-media, and donation links lead to third-party services. When you follow an external link, that service's privacy policy applies. Solar Time does not receive your donation or social-account credentials.

5. Data sharing and retention

Life User does not sell personal information. Technical information may be processed by hosting, analytics, security, and advertising providers only for their stated services, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or protection of rights. Retention is controlled by those providers and by operational security needs. Local preferences remain until you clear site data or use the in-app reset options.

6. Children

Solar Time is a general-audience educational visualization and is not directed specifically to children under the age required to consent to data processing in their country. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

7. Your choices and contact

You may clear local site data in your browser, disable optional music, decline advertising consent where offered, or stop using the service. For privacy questions or requests, contact Lyrikey@Naver.com. This policy may be updated when the service or its providers change; the date at the top identifies the current version.
